# Product Vision

## Purpose

Lumière is an active French-learning application. It turns authentic French material and exam practice into repeated, targeted output: reading and listening lead to retrieval, writing, speaking, conjugation, review, and feedback.

The product is designed for an independent learner who values durable progress over passive consumption, gamification, or social comparison.

## Core loop

1. Study French through a document, TCF question set, vocabulary entry, or speaking prompt.
2. Capture learning signals: lookups, answers, writing feedback, pronunciation results, and self-identified vocabulary gaps.
3. Use those signals to select or generate the next practice activity.
4. Preserve attempts and feedback so review and progress views reveal recurring weaknesses.

## Product principles

- **Active output first.** Features should create meaningful production or retrieval, not merely make content easier to consume.
- **Errors are learning signals.** Classify, explain, and revisit errors without using rankings, streak pressure, or punitive scoring.
- **Context matters.** Vocabulary and feedback should retain a link to the source or exercise that produced them.
- **Personal data stays personal.** Learning history is private and is always scoped to its owner in production.
- **AI is bounded.** AI helps explain, generate, and assess; deterministic sources are used where an exercise teaches a fixed answer.

## Current product areas

- Library and Reader: personal French documents, reading sessions, contextual vocabulary lookup, and reading progress.
- Vocabulary: lemma-based entries, saved words, source occurrences, vocabulary gaps, and a review queue.
- Practice: source-anchored writing tasks, structured feedback, archived errors, and micro-drills.
- TCF Canada: reading and listening drills, complete exams, attempt history, review queues, explanations, and vocabulary-gap capture.
- Quiz and Cloze: imported objective-question sets and podcast-style cloze exercises.
- Conjugation: deterministic French verb drills, informed by learner history.
- Speaking: TCF speaking prompts, generated scripts, recording, and Azure pronunciation assessment when configured.
- Progress and Today: cross-feature practice signals, trends, readiness, and next actions.

## Deliberate boundaries

- Lumière is not a social network, leaderboard, badge system, or passive-content platform.
- Public demos must not expose private learning data or invoke paid AI services for anonymous visitors.
- The app does not treat historical design documents as current requirements.
